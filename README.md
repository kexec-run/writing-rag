# writing-rag

A local writing-memory prototype that turns chat and transcript exports into
searchable source material. It keeps conversational context separate from
examples of your own writing and retains source references for review.

> **Work in progress.** Import, retrieval, article generation, and verification
> are implemented. The research explores conversation structure, evidence
> selection, author-guided scaffolding, and preservation of causal flow.

```text
selected local exports
  -> canonical messages and conversation windows
  -> content_memory + self-only style_memory
  -> Qwen retrieval
  -> grounded context for local article generation and verification
```

## What is implemented

- Import adapters for Instagram, WhatsApp, Reddit/Matrix-style chat, Whisper,
  ChatGPT, and Twitter inputs.
- Canonical messages with source, thread, timestamp, speaker, and provenance.
- Structure-aware conversation windows that retain message IDs and time bounds.
- Separate content and style collections; only self-authored messages enter the
  style collection.
- Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B as the corpus retrieval model. Jina is not a corpus
  retriever; its text-matching and reranking roles are isolated experiments over
  Qwen-provided candidates.
- Local generation with evidence and claim checks. Article quality and causal
  narrative reconstruction remain areas for evaluation; the synthetic demo is
  plumbing validation, not a semantic-quality result.

## Selected research findings

The project includes model comparisons, controlled ablations, and generation
trials. The findings below are reported results from the documented studies;
private-corpus measurements have not been rerun as part of this public release.

| Study | Recorded result | Interpretation and public summary |
|---|---|---|
| Jina symmetric text matching | MRR **0.8500**, AP **0.8458**, **14/20** rank-1 positives, five hard-negative failures. | Direction supported on the small frozen matching pilot; auxiliary use only. [Summary](docs/experiments/studies/study-014.md) |
| Qwen retrieval with bounded Jina selection | MRR **0.8000 → 0.8500**; nDCG@10 **0.8586 → 0.8884**. | Selection improved within the Qwen pool; both generated articles remained rejected. [Summary](docs/experiments/studies/study-026.md) |
| Jina reranker confirmation | On **110 unseen anchors**, Recall@10 **0.7364 → 0.8182** and Recall@5 **0.6273 → 0.7182**; **15 rescues / 6 damages**. | Aggregate gains, but the p99 band regressed by 10 percentage points and uncertainty bounds were inadequate. Decision: `not_confirmed`. [Summary](docs/experiments/studies/study-011.md) |
| Two-pass article distillation | **10/10 section checks passed** in the recorded O0 typed v6 run. | The continuity rewrite truncated and failed validation; the workflow retained the validated section draft as its fallback. [Summary](docs/experiments/studies/study-036.md) |
| MDX source availability | All **six conditions** selected the same top ten base chunks and generated byte-identical outputs. | Added sources were indexed but ranked below the usable cutoff. This isolated a ranking limit in that test. [Summary](docs/experiments/studies/study-017.md) |
| Shape–Beat validation, synthetic demo | Two invented cases each pass **22 checks**, including **15 negative mutations**. | Demonstrates schema, compilation, scheduling, and failure checks; it is separate from the historical generation pilot. [Run it](experiments/active/shape-beat-axis0-v1/README.md#public-synthetic-validation) |

MRR measures where the first relevant result appears; AP summarizes relevant
result ordering. Recall@k measures relevant material recovered within the first
k results, and nDCG@10 accounts for relevance and order within the first ten.
These metrics are interpreted within each study's own cases and relevance
definitions. They are not article-quality scores.

See the [decision ledger](docs/architecture/experiment-ledger.md) for the wider
set of model and architecture decisions, including negative findings.

This table distinguishes completed comparisons from partial studies. The linked
summaries record the available controls and limitations; private plans, cases,
and raw traces are not included.

| Comparison | What changed and scope | Recorded finding | Public summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative scaffold structure | Amount and type of narrative guidance, including B2-lite; target-specific scaffold trials. | B2-lite had the fewest validation issues in the corrected comparison, but all article variants remained rejected. No minimum scaffold was established. | [Scaffold study](docs/experiments/studies/study-023.md) |
| Near-black scaffold wording | Standard and low-abstraction scaffold approaches, explanations, and writing trials. | The public summary does not establish a winning wording variant. Section distillation passed, while continuity failed and used a fallback. | [Near-black trials](docs/experiments/studies/study-020.md) |
| Scaffold and text selection | Scaffold/selector combinations using Qwen and bounded Jina selection. | Corrected v2: B2-lite had 3 issues, full B2 with Jina had 6, and the load-bearing semantic condition had 17. All variants were rejected; these issue counts do not isolate a selector-only effect. | [Combined ablation](docs/experiments/studies/study-030.md) |
| MDX source availability | Additional source articles under a fixed target-derived scaffold. | Six conditions produced the same selected top ten chunks and byte-identical outputs. Added prose remained below the usable rank cutoff. | [Source ablation](docs/experiments/studies/study-017.md) |
| MDX holdouts | Target-only versus all-MDX holdout comparison; the run was interrupted. | Partial result only. Neither holdout policy was established as better. | [Holdout study](docs/experiments/studies/study-031.md) |
| Whole-query versus per-slot retrieval | W0 global retrieval, S0 per-slot retrieval, and S1 reranking inside the S0 pools. | All articles were rejected. | Article-plan drafting context | Bounds and representations of source-specific drafting context. | The consolidated public record does not establish a general article-quality gain or separate quantitative outcomes for every variant. | [Context studies](docs/experiments/studies/study-001.md) |
| Shape–Beat composition | Flat obligations, Shape units, Beat units, and gates in bounded pilot work. | Schema checks passed; generation exposed source-evidence and verifier boundaries. The two oracle cases were a pilot, not confirmation. | [Composition study](docs/experiments/studies/study-033.md) |
| Qwen query encoding | Current Q0 versus card-style Q1 query encoding on the frozen pilot. | MRR was 0.8000 versus 0.7750. Q0 remained the measured baseline; this does not establish a general rule against query instructions. | [Encoding comparison](docs/experiments/studies/study-027.md) |

## Try the synthetic demo

Requires Python 3.11 or later:

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
python -m pip install -e .
python scripts/run_synthetic_demo.py
```

The demo uses invented source-shaped fixtures and explicit hashing. It imports
messages, builds conversation windows and separate content/style documents,
retrieves examples, and assembles a generation context. It does not download or
run a model. This validates data plumbing only; it does not measure semantic
retrieval quality or article quality. It refuses to run if `data/raw/` contains
anything except the exact synthetic fixtures. Generated artifacts stay under
ignored `data/`.

Two additional model-free experiment demonstrations are available:

- [Frozen ranking replay](experiments/active/qwen-top50-reranker-v2/README.md)
  exercises ranking metrics on invented candidate pools; it does not run Qwen
  or Jina and makes no model-quality claim.
- [Shape–Beat validation](experiments/active/shape-beat-axis0-v1/README.md)
  validates two synthetic composition cases and their negative mutations; it
  does not reproduce the private pilot or establish article-generation quality.

## Run with local models

Install the optional model/runtime dependencies and make the Qwen embedding
model available in the Hugging Face cache. This operational path also requires
your own imported corpus and locally generated retrieval documents/vectors; it
is not one of the clean-clone synthetic demonstrations above. Without those
inputs and weights, the model pipeline is not expected to run:

```bash
python -m pip install -r requirements-qwen.txt
export WRITING_RAG_MODEL_CACHE="$PWD/models/weights/huggingface"
python scripts/run_pipeline.py --embedding-backend qwen3
```

The cache path should be a Hugging Face cache root with its normal `hub/` and
snapshot layout. Override it for your machine using
`WRITING_RAG_MODEL_CACHE`. Model weights and all corpus-derived artifacts are
local machine state and must not be committed. The repository's MIT license
does not change the separate licenses that apply to model weights.

## Limitations and recorded validation

Article generation is implemented, but ranking improvements have not yet
established repeatable article acceptance across the tested scaffold comparisons.
The model-free demonstrations validate their specific contracts. Historical
corpus experiments require private inputs or dependencies described in their
summaries.

### Validation snapshot

Experiments are kept separate from the canonical Qwen retrieval path. Published
summaries must distinguish implemented behavior from exploratory results and
must not include corpus payloads or private evaluation traces. Reproducing
corpus-based metrics requires the private source corpus; this public snapshot
contains only synthetic fixtures. The initial public seed's validation counts
(154 passing tests and 181 Ruff findings) are historical. For the current
release candidate, the default suite reported 177 passed, 33 private-data
skips, and 6 subtests passed. The documentation validator passed 21 negative
fixtures. Repository-wide Ruff reported 179 findings, with no new file/rule
diagnostics relative to the reviewed initial commit; the changed D1/D2 Python
files are clean. The D1 ranking replay and both D2 synthetic cases passed, and
repeated D1 rankings and D2 outputs matched exactly. These checks do not make
the repository lint-clean or validate semantic-model or article quality. A
separate optional experiment test collection still has two import errors
because excluded local Qwen characterization modules are unavailable;
private-data skips and that optional collection blocker are not hidden by the
passing default suite.

### Research inventory

The public [experiment catalog](docs/experiments/README.md) records 38 research
lines, 25 named experiment directories, 96 excluded Python support or experiment
files, and 222 held artifact directories. These are inventory counts, not
independent runs or samples. Each study separates implementation disposition
from scientific outcome and marks results transcribed from the private decision
ledger.

### Reproduction limits

An optional experiment-test collection produced two errors because excluded
local Qwen characterization modules are unavailable. Static inspection found
11 imports from public experiment/test files that resolve only to excluded local
modules. Some runners also require private corpora or generated artifacts. The
catalog records known blockers; this release step did not restore those paths.

### Current research direction

The canonical retrieval path uses Qwen. Jina text matching remains auxiliary,
and the Jina reranker result is unconfirmed. Ranking gains have not yet produced
a repeatable accepted article across the tested scaffold comparisons. See the
[current architecture](docs/architecture/single-path.md), [model roles](docs/architecture/qwen-jina-reranker-tree.md), and [article-generation problem brief](docs/architecture/article-generation-problem-brief.md).
