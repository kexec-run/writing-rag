# Article planning and scoped draft context

Study ID: `study-001`

Disposition: `results-only`
Decision: experimental; no article-plan path is promoted as the default.

## Question

Can an explicit plan and bounded source context improve article drafting while preserving evidence and author intent?

## Method and controls

This study record consolidates a research line from the private experiment history. It covers **19 held artifact directories**; that count is an inventory count, not an execution count, a sample size or a count of independent cases. Individual payloads were not copied into the public release.

## Findings

Several article-plan and draft-context variants were explored. Publicly available materials are incomplete for reproducing their source-specific comparisons; the current evidence does not establish a general article-quality improvement.

## Evidence and limits

Private outlines, source plans, assertions and per-target payloads are excluded. Do not infer a general win from an individual accepted or rejected draft. No sanitized source result was available for a quantitative claim in this pass.
