# Near-black article scaffolds and writing trials

Study ID: `study-020`

Disposition: `results-only`
Decision: retain the evidence-first fallback pattern; article generation remains unresolved.

## Question

Can a low-abstraction scaffold preserve evidence, causality and a conversational public voice?

## Method and controls

This study record consolidates a research line from the private experiment history. It covers **12 held artifact directories**; that count is an inventory count, not an execution count, a sample size or a count of independent cases. Individual payloads were not copied into the public release.

## Findings

Near-black scaffold variants did not yield an accepted article under the recorded validation contracts. The two-pass distillation run passed section-level checks but failed continuity and fell back to the validated section draft. A separate Qwythos test confirmed exact-once artifact-marker placement and valid figure-link syntax on six outputs; this narrow formatting result did not establish claim grounding.

## Evidence and limits

These are target-specific writing trials, not evidence that all scaffolding fails. Source plans and article drafts are excluded. See the [public decision ledger](../../architecture/experiment-ledger.md); exact source-line provenance is retained in the private inventory.
