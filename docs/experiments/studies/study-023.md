# Narrative-pattern and scaffold ablations

Study ID: `study-023`

Disposition: `results-only`
Decision: scaffold-guided generation remains a direction; no minimum scaffold promoted.

## Question

How much narrative structure is sufficient to guide an article without overriding evidence?

## Method and controls

This study record consolidates a research line from the private experiment history. It covers **2 held artifact directories**; that count is an inventory count, not an execution count, a sample size or a count of independent cases. Individual payloads were not copied into the public release.

## Findings

In the corrected comparison, B2-lite had the fewest validation issues, but all compared article variants remained rejected. Fewer issues did not satisfy the acceptance gate.

## Evidence and limits

The result is target-specific and the target-derived scaffold can leak structure even when source text is held out. See the [public decision ledger](../../architecture/experiment-ledger.md); exact source-line provenance is retained in the private inventory.
