NEAR-BLACK DISTILLATION V1
==========================

The input is a segmented evidence scaffold. Convert it into public-facing
Markdown/MDX without changing the evidence relationships.

The scaffold contains three registers:

- Strict wording: source-faithful observations, measurements, hypotheses, and
  decisions.
- Low-abstraction explanation: concrete explanations of the strict wording.
- Causal link: the smallest justified movement to the next section.

The public output must not print these labels. Use them as internal drafting
material. A technical word may be followed by a plain-language explanation,
but the explanation must not replace or strengthen the technical claim.

Pass one: section distillation
------------------------------

Write one to three continuous paragraphs for the supplied section. Preserve
the section's order, all supported measurements and qualifiers, and its causal
status. Use only the supplied section evidence. Do not introduce a new fact,
measurement, causal relation, figure, or file path.

Use these distinctions accurately:

- residual: the remaining difference after the source is locally resized to
  the WhatsApp output dimensions;
- control: the 1600x900 source that remains 1600x900 after WhatsApp;
- fair comparison: local downscale versus WhatsApp output at the same size;
- vertical bias: column-profile standard deviation minus row-profile standard
  deviation;
- FFT: a frequency representation of spatial patterns;
- FFT(residual diff): the FFT of the spatial residual image, not
  FFT(WhatsApp) - FFT(source).

Keep “about”, “approximately”, “on an 8-bit scale”, “current sample set”, and
similar qualifiers when the source scaffold uses them. State hypotheses as
hypotheses. Do not claim WhatsApp's private filter, settings, server boundary,
or implementation.

Pass two: continuity distillation
---------------------------------

Combine the accepted section drafts into one public article. Keep the section
headings and order. Improve transitions, paragraph rhythm, and readability,
but do not add factual content. Do not remove a measurement or qualifier to
make the prose smoother. Do not add a figure or reproduction path.

The final article must:

- contain exactly the two selected comparison figures;
- contain all three reproduction paths;
- include the fair comparison and the control result;
- explain FFT(residual diff) correctly;
- preserve the current-sample-set limitation;
- end with a complete sentence;
- contain no scaffold labels, source IDs, retrieval metadata, model names, or
  prompt references.

Return only the requested prose. Do not return a plan, audit, JSON, or
explanation of the generation process.
